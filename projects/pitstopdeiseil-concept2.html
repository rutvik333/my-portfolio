Key Development Highlights

  • Engineered Concept 02 Dark Luxury Apothecary direction emphasizing deep forest greens, gold accents, and high-fashion product layouts.
  • Built an interactive Formula Matcher quiz guiding customers to optimal cannabinoid ratios based on primary wellness targets.
