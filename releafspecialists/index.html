Requirements

Do I qualify for a medical marijuana card?

Every state keeps its own list of qualifying conditions. In Pennsylvania they're called "serious medical conditions." We'll help you work out where you stand.

Not sure your condition counts?

If you think you have a qualifying condition, go ahead and book. The physician will go over it with you on your call. Already registered with your state? Keep your patient ID number handy, since it's in your state profile. If you haven't registered yet, send us a copy of your driver's license instead.

No medical records?

Living with anxiety or PTSD, but never been diagnosed?

Plenty of people manage anxiety or PTSD on their own without ever getting a formal diagnosis. If that sounds like you, our physicians can evaluate you for Generalized Anxiety Disorder or PTSD, and a diagnosis may qualify you for your card. Start with the free assessment and see where you land.
